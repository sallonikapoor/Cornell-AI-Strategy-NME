1. 01
    Ask about the last time, not "usually." Real examples are more accurate than general descriptions.
  2. 02
    Get numbers. "A lot of emails" becomes "about 120 a week."
  3. 03
    Find where the data lives. An AI tool can only use information that exists somewhere it can reach.
  4. 04
    Find who feels the pain. The person who asked isn't always the person most affected.
  5. 05
    Hold off on solutions. Once you start pitching, the client stops telling you things.
  6. 06
    Summarize it back. "So the main problem is… did I get that right?"
